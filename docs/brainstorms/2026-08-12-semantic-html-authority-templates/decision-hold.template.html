Close and supersession rules

  1. Never edit a hold to look as though it never existed; close it with a dated successor decision and retained link.
  2. Closing one blocker may narrow a hold but cannot promote unrelated truth stages.
  3. Expiry without owner review leaves lifecycle held and records unresolved expiry in a separate role-specific field; it never implies approved.
  4. A decision that changes an accepted baseline must identify the old and new baseline hashes and allocate trace/V&V records.
  5. Historical evidence retains its original baseline and observation; a successor decision may accept or reject newer-baseline applicability but may not rewrite the old record.
  6. Publication claims remain held until the exact reviewed tree, PR/merge state, release state, and deployed state are independently evidenced.
